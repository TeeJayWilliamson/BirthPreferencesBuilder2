About Blümi
Blümi is a guided birth planning tool built on evidence-based information. It helps birthers understand their options, find their voice, and create a personalized birth plan they can share with their entire care team, so everyone arrives prepared.
Blümi is for anyone navigating pregnancy and birth, whether it's your first baby or your fourth. It's also built for the providers who support them: doulas, midwives, and OBs who want their clients to arrive informed and ready for a real conversation.
A free template gives you checkboxes. Blümi gives you a fully guided experience:
  • Evidence-based information is woven into every step so your choices are truly informed, not just filled in.
  • It's built around your voice. Blümi surfaces your values and communication style so the plan you create actually sounds like you.
  • It's made to be shared. Export a beautifully formatted plan and send it directly to your care team so everyone arrives aligned.
Yes. Every piece of information in Blümi is grounded in current research and aligned with respectful maternity care principles. We don't offer opinions, we offer informed options. We also cite our sources, so you can always read further if you want to.
Blümi is launching first in Canada and the United States. We're working to expand access. Sign up for the waitlist to be notified when Blümi becomes available in your region.
We believe every birther deserves access to information in the language they're most comfortable in. French and Spanish versions are in the works. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know as soon as they're available.
For Families
You don't need one. Blümi is built so anyone navigating pregnancy can use it on their own. You can share the plan you create with whatever care team supports you, whether that's a doula, midwife, OB, or all three.
We recommend starting around 28–32 weeks. That gives you enough time to explore your options thoughtfully and have meaningful conversations with your care team, with room to update as your pregnancy evolves. It's also never too late. Even a plan created close to your due date is better than none at all.
Birth is unpredictable, and that's okay. A birth plan isn't about controlling what happens, it's about knowing yourself well enough to navigate whatever does. Even when plans change, you'll still know:
  • What questions to ask
  • What matters most to you
  • That your care team already understands your wishes
That preparation doesn't disappear when plans change. It's what helps you feel informed and supported in the moments that matter most, even the unplanned ones.
Yes. Blümi is built for every birth setting, whether that's a hospital, birth centre, or home. Your values and preferences matter regardless of where you give birth, and Blümi helps you articulate them clearly so your care team knows exactly how to support you.
Yes, and you're not alone in asking. Blümi won't erase what happened before. But it can help you reclaim your voice for this birth, giving you the information, language, and space to communicate what you need, what you're worried about, and what matters most to you this time. We also gently encourage anyone navigating birth trauma to seek support from a trained professional alongside Blümi.
Yes. Your birth plan is yours to update whenever you need to. As your pregnancy evolves, conversations with your care team happen, or your preferences change, you can come back and revise it. Progress is saved automatically so you can always pick up where you left off.
Once your plan is complete, you can export it as a beautifully formatted PDF and share it directly with your care team by email, print, or however works best for your provider.
For Providers
Providers get access to a dedicated portal where you can manage your client list, review birth plans, and communicate with families. Clients you add can build their plans within your practice's Blümi space, and you'll be notified when their plan is ready to review.
Yes. Our Practice and Collective tiers support multi-provider teams under one account. Each provider gets their own login and PIN, and an Admin role can manage the team and handle unassigned clients.
Archiving a client immediately frees up their slot, so your plan count always reflects your active caseload. Archived clients and their plans are still accessible in your records.
Customization options vary by tier. Higher-tier plans include expanded tools for tailoring the client experience within your practice. See the Pricing page for a full breakdown.
You'll receive notifications through the portal when clients complete or update their plans. We're continuing to build out the communication tools based on provider feedback.
