Questions
Everything you need to know.
Is there a free trial?
+
Yes — Personal comes with a 14-day free trial, and all three Professional tiers include a 14-day trial too. No credit card required to get started on any plan.
What does my client actually get when I invite them?
+
Your client gets full access to Blümi — the guided birth plan builder, AI story generator, PDF downloads, and the shared portal with you — completely free, for as long as they're an active client in your account. It's a genuine perk you offer, not a watered-down version.
What happens to a client's plan when I archive them?
+
Their plan becomes read-only — they can view and download it anytime, but can't make new edits. If they want to keep editing (say, for a future pregnancy), they can subscribe to Personal and the plan carries over seamlessly. No data is ever lost.
Can I switch Professional tiers later?
+
Absolutely. Upgrade anytime and it takes effect immediately (we pro-rate the difference). Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period — we'll let you know if any active client slots would be affected.
Is my clients' data private and secure?
+
Yes. Birth plans contain sensitive personal health information and we take that seriously. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, never sold, and never used for advertising. Only the client, and any birth workers they've been connected to, can access a plan.
Do you offer discounts for student doulas?
+
We do! Students enrolled in a certified doula training programme can apply for 50% off Solo Professional for up to 12 months. Email hello@blumi.co with proof of enrolment and we'll sort you out.
