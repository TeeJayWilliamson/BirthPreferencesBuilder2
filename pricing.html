Questions
Everything you need to know.
What does my client get when I invite them?
+
Full access. The guided birth plan builder, Intelligent story generator, PDF downloads, and the shared portal with you. It's the complete product, free for your clients for as long as they're active in your account.
What happens to a client's plan when I archive them?
+
Their plan becomes read-only. They can view and download it anytime, but can't make new edits. If they want to keep editing (say, for a future pregnancy), they can subscribe to Personal and the plan carries over seamlessly. No data is ever lost.
Can I switch Professional tiers?
+
Absolutely. Upgrade anytime and it takes effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.
Is my clients' data private and secure?
+
Yes. Birth plans contain sensitive personal health information and we take that seriously. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, never sold, and never used for advertising. Only the client and their connected Care Provider can access a plan.
Do you offer discounts for student doulas?
+
We do! Students enrolled in a certified doula training programme can apply for 50% off Solo for up to 12 months. Email hello@blumibirth.com with proof of enrolment and we'll sort you out.
